Preparedness playbook

Interactive disaster management guidance

Every section expands with practical actions so visitors can move from awareness to response.

  • Anchor heavy shelves, water tanks, and appliances before seismic activity begins.
  • Practice drop, cover, and hold on with everyone in the household.
  • Keep shoes, torch, power bank, and essential medicine in a go-bag near exits.
  • Track rainfall, river updates, and route closures before travel into low-lying areas.
  • Move documents, electronics, and backup food above expected flood height.
  • Never walk or drive through moving water, even when the depth looks shallow.
  • Secure outdoor furniture, loose roofing materials, and drainage around the home.
  • Store offline copies of emergency contacts and identify the safest indoor room.
  • Charge devices early and prepare for power disruption and transport delays.
  • Create a clear zone around buildings by removing dry vegetation and debris.
  • Keep masks, first aid supplies, and evacuation papers ready during active fire seasons.
  • Leave early when evacuation alerts are issued because smoke and road closures can spread fast.

Response step 1

Assess the signal

Compare weather severity, hazard feed location, and timing before taking action.

Response step 2

Share the plan

Tell family or team members where to meet, who to call, and what route stays safest.

Response step 3

Move with essentials

Carry water, identity documents, medicine, cash, and device backup before leaving.

Response step 4

Recheck conditions

Use live feeds again after movement because hazards can change by the hour.
