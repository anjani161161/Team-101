Management framework

Disaster response flow

Use the same response rhythm across every disaster section.

Step 1

Verify the source

Confirm event timing, location, and source link before distributing alerts to others.

Step 2

Map the impact

Check whether homes, transport routes, schools, or utilities fall inside the risk area.

Step 3

Activate response

Move people, supplies, and communications based on the hazard category and severity.

Step 4

Keep monitoring

Re-check the live sections because active incidents can expand, close, or change location.
