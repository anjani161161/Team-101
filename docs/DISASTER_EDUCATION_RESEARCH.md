# Disaster Education Research and Source Register

Reviewed: 2026-10-28. This register documents source review for the AtmosWatch Disaster Learning Hub and Disaster Watch feed. AtmosWatch content is educational and is not an official approval, emergency warning, site inspection, or substitute for local plans.

## Sources reviewed

- [NDMA SACHET Dos and Don'ts](https://sachet.ndma.gov.in/DosDont): current hazard index includes cyclones, tsunamis, heat waves, lightning, floods, earthquakes, urban floods, landslides, fire, drought, forest fire, biological emergencies, and chemical emergencies. SACHET lists public videos, but did not provide a stable, independently verified per-video title/ID and reuse record for all desired scenarios during this implementation.
- [NDRF Disaster Awareness](https://www.ndrf.gov.in/en/disaster-awareness): links official multilingual YouTube playlists for preparedness, earthquake, landslide, flood, tsunami, cyclone, thunder/lightning, drought, and heat wave, among other subjects. These are outbound references only; AtmosWatch did not embed individual external videos because each target's exact subject, availability, captions, and embedding/reuse conditions were not independently validated.
- [NDRF School Safety and Mock Exercise](https://www.ndrf.gov.in/en/school-safety-and-mock-exercise): reviewed school disaster plans, evacuation, psychosocial care, school disaster-management committees, exercise planning, and mock-drill guidance.
- [NIDM Awareness Materials](https://nidm.mha.gov.in/iec.asp): reviewed common-disaster, zone-wise, multilingual and student awareness resources.
- [NIDM Self Study Programme](https://nidm.mha.gov.in/ssp.asp): reviewed its listed learning subjects, including flood, cyclone, health care, landslide, school safety, earthquake, and industrial risk.
- [India Meteorological Department](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/): linked as the official source for current weather and cyclone/heat guidance; local current bulletins take precedence.
- [INCOIS](https://incois.gov.in/): linked for tsunami and ocean information; follow local coastal evacuation instructions.
- [USGS Earthquake Hazards](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards): reviewed as an additional earthquake and tsunami science reference.
- [Ready.gov](https://www.ready.gov/): reviewed as supplemental public preparedness guidance; U.S.-specific directions are not presented as Indian local policy.
- [National Weather Service Lightning Safety](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning): supplemental lightning guidance; local IMD and authority instructions govern.
- [National Fire Protection Association public fire safety](https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/home-fire-safety): supplemental general fire education, not an Indian code reference.
- [NASA Earth Observatory RSS](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/feeds/image-of-the-day.rss): current published stories and source-provided image content used for the public feed.
- [NASA media use guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/): reviewed. NASA allows factual educational/informational use subject to attribution and other guidelines, but individual third-party material identified by NASA remains subject to its rights holder's terms.
- [GDACS API Swagger](https://www.gdacs.org/gdacsapi/swagger/index.html) and [GDACS terms](https://www.gdacs.org/documents/2025/GDACS_Terms_of_use_Mar_25.pdf): public event metadata; acknowledge “Global Disaster Awareness and Coordination System, GDACS” and retain event links. Event data are not local official warnings or a complete photo archive.
- [ReliefWeb API documentation](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/): read API requests require an approved `appname`; no placeholder is used. No application name was provided, so ReliefWeb is not queried or represented as integrated. Report/image third-party rights remain with original publishers.

## Disaster categories and learning objectives

Each pathway contains hazard understanding, causes, warning signals, misconceptions, regional caveats, before/during/after guidance, evacuation or shelter guidance, campus roles, accessibility, common mistakes, recovery, a server-scored checkpoint, and links to source publishers.

| Category                             | Learning objective                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Earthquakes                          | Protect oneself during shaking, then follow a safe, planned post-shaking response; consider aftershocks and coastal tsunami instructions. |
| Floods and urban flooding            | Recognize changing water hazards, avoid all floodwater crossings, and use authority-directed safe routes and re-entry.                    |
| Fire and building fires              | Raise the alarm, use a safe planned route, account for the group, and never re-enter without clearance.                                   |
| Cyclones and severe storms           | Monitor official warnings, evacuate early when ordered, shelter appropriately, and avoid surge and post-storm hazards.                    |
| Lightning and thunderstorms          | Stop outdoor activity and seek substantial enclosed shelter; resume only after the local stand-down period.                               |
| Landslides and debris flows          | Recognize possible slope warning signs, move away from slide paths when safe, and avoid affected slopes and channels.                     |
| Heatwaves and extreme temperatures   | Reduce heat exposure, use institution plans, and obtain prompt adult/medical help for serious symptoms.                                   |
| Wildfires and smoke                  | Follow fire authority instructions, evacuate via confirmed routes, limit smoke exposure, and await all-clear.                             |
| Tsunamis and coastal hazards         | Respond immediately to natural or official warnings by moving inland/uphill and remain until the official all-clear.                      |
| Drought and water scarcity           | Use only approved safe water, follow restrictions, and maintain accessible school water/sanitation continuity.                            |
| Chemical and laboratory incidents    | Do not touch or identify unknown substances; follow incident-specific evacuation or shelter instructions.                                 |
| Biological/public-health emergencies | Follow current health authority and school procedures, protect privacy, and avoid diagnosis, stigma, and rumours.                         |
| Industrial/infrastructure incidents  | Follow incident-specific authority directions, which may require shelter or evacuation; avoid damaged facilities and utilities.           |
| Emergency evacuation/campus safety   | Use incident-specific accessible plans, group accountability, communication, reunification, and authorized re-entry.                      |

## Verified safety guidance applied

- Protective actions are hazard-specific. The user interface deliberately distinguishes shelter from evacuation; a generic evacuation rule is not applied to all incidents.
- Do not enter moving floodwater, unknown chemical areas, smoke, damaged structures, active slide areas, or restricted infrastructure.
- Use official alerts and approved institution plans. Site/building conditions, disability access, local geography, event intensity, and responder instructions can change the correct action.
- Keep students with responsible adults, account for groups, report injury/hazard from safety, and wait for official all-clear before return.
- Health, chemical, industrial, and structural emergencies require current qualified instructions; the learning text is not a diagnosis or a technical response procedure.
- NDRF, NDMA, NIDM, IMD, INCOIS, USGS, WHO/NCDC, and other linked references are displayed as their own publishers, not as AtmosWatch endorsements.

## Videos and original animated scenarios

- Verified external videos embedded: **0**. The NDRF and SACHET awareness pages were reviewed, but the individual outbound video targets were not all verified for current availability, exact content, captions, and embedding/reuse permissions. No video IDs, durations, or publisher metadata were fabricated.
- Original AtmosWatch animated scenario lessons: **70** (14 categories × 5 distinct scenarios each). Each has a situation, decision prompt, hazard-specific action, rationale, visible step transcript, play/pause/restart controls, reduced-motion CSS behavior, and a server-checked knowledge checkpoint. These are original CSS/React educational illustrations, not incident footage.
- External video entries remain available only as outbound official source links in topic references. If curated videos are added later, verify each page, title, creator, language, captions, embed permission, and reuse terms before publishing.

## Public photo and article feed

- NASA Earth Observatory RSS supplies genuine published story title/summary, publisher link, publication timestamp, and source-provided image/caption when present. The feed filters for disaster-related categories and terms; it is not described as live video. Image display requires an approved NASA-hosted HTTPS URL and a source caption; an image-specific rights caveat links readers to the original story.
- The public GDACS integration supplies normalized event metadata and source links separately from articles. It has no claim to provide event photography. Event occurrence/update times are labeled separately from story publication dates.
- Upstream results are server-cached for 20 minutes. The public response exposes last successful update, partial/stale status, source status, and source errors. Refresh does not fetch upstream data on every React render.
- No ReliefWeb query is made until an approved `appname` is configured and the integration is explicitly enabled; there is no invalid placeholder or repeated failing request.

## Attribution, reuse, and limitations

- NASA images are not assumed to be unrestricted merely because NASA hosts them. The feed keeps image caption/credit, displays the story link, excludes uncredited or unapproved image hosts, and tells readers to check the original story for third-party rights. This implementation does not download or bundle third-party images.
- GDACS is acknowledged and linked; its alert/event metadata are not promoted as a local government warning.
- NDRF/NIDM/NDMA source resources are linked, not copied wholesale. Their individual copyright/website policies apply.
- Topic text is concise learning content adapted from these source families and reviewed for general safety principles; it is not a verbatim reproduction, expert certification, site-specific plan, or real-time advice. Translation, local review, subject-matter review, and scheduled editorial verification are still needed before deployment to students.
- Stories can be empty, unavailable, or stale when upstream feeds fail or publish no matching item. Current implementation uses NASA Earth Observatory and GDACS only. ReliefWeb, state authority article feeds, photo detail pages, and a curriculum-admin publishing workflow are not implemented.
